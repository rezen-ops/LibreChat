# KMH brand voice

Consult `references/banned-phrases.md` before drafting any subject line or preview text.
Segment names must match `references/klaviyo-segments.csv` exactly — never invent one.

## Subject lines
- 32–42 characters.
- Never use an em dash.
- Never open with "Introducing", "We're excited to", or "Don't miss".
- Sentence case, not title case.

## Body copy
- Second person. Active voice.
- One idea per paragraph, maximum three sentences.
- No exclamation marks outside of a quoted customer testimonial.

## Reporting language
When summarising campaign performance, always state the comparison window
explicitly ("vs. the previous 30 days"), never just "up 12%".
